- The original `.HEIC` files **cannot** be uploaded (HEIC is not an allowed type). The JPG previews or new exports must be used.
- Dimensions (confirmed with `sips`): IMG_2078, 2875, 4071 and 4107 are stored as 1200 × 1600. IMG_4088, 4089, 4101 and 5643 are stored as **1600 × 1200**, yet they display as portrait. Their pixels are saved sideways and rotated by an EXIF orientation flag. Browsers apply the flag, but any tool that strips EXIF (resizing, WEBP export, a future Cloudinary transform) would show them sideways. Before uploading, re-export them with the rotation applied to the pixels.
- 1200 × 1600 is enough for a product page, but lower than the iPhone originals (usually 3024 × 4032). For zoom-quality media, re-export from HEIC at about 2000 px on the long edge.
- In every HEIC shot, props (dolls, stethoscope, tags, sketch cards, bag, notebook) overlap the garment. None of them is a clean ghost-mannequin source without retouching.
